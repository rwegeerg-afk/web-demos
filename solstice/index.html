The grounds

Eleven hectares of meadow on the east shore. From the entrance to the water is a nine-minute walk.

Good to know

How do I get there?

Free shuttle buses leave the central station every 15 minutes from 12:00, and run back until 03:00. The ride takes about 40 minutes.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. Weekend and camping wristbands allow re-entry at any time. Day passes allow one re-entry before 20:00.

Is there an age limit?

Under 16s need an adult with them on site. Children under 12 enter free with a ticketed adult.

Can I swim in the lake?

At the marked beach between 10:00 and 19:00, when lifeguards are on duty. The pier around the Lake Stage is closed to swimmers.
