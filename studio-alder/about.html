01About

A small studio that draws a lot.

Studio Alder is a fictional office of three people. We keep the team small so that the person you talk to is the person who draws.

02Principles

What we hold to.

Three ideas guide every plan, from a garden room to a house.

  1. Plan before picture

    If the plan is clear, the building will be easy to live in. We show drawings first and renderings only when they help a decision.

  2. Fewer, better rooms

    Space is expensive. We would rather give you one generous room than three that you walk through.

  3. Say what it costs

    Each option comes with a plain note on its effect on cost, light and building time.
