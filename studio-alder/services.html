01Services

Four kinds of work, one way of working.

All services are for homes and small commercial spaces. Timings and what is included are examples for this demo, not an offer.

A

New houses

Typical: 6–10 months of design

A house designed around your plot, your budget and the way you live, from first sketch to drawings for the builder.

  • Plot and orientation study
  • Three layout options
  • Planning application drawings
  • Construction drawings
  • Material and window schedule
  • Site visits during the build

B

Extensions

Typical: 3–5 months of design

More room without losing what is good about the house you have. We draw the existing plan first, then show what changes.

  • Measured survey of the existing house
  • Existing and new plan on one sheet
  • Light and view study
  • Drawings for permits
  • Detail drawings for the builder
  • Help comparing quotes

C

Renovation

Typical: 2–4 months of design

Moving a stair, opening a kitchen, enlarging a bathroom: small changes in plan that make the whole home work better.

  • Review of what works and what does not
  • Plan changes drawn and discussed
  • Services and wall-removal checklist
  • Kitchen and bathroom layouts
  • Phasing if you live in the house
  • Check on site before finishes

D

Interior layouts

Typical: 4–8 weeks of design

Furniture, storage and circulation planned room by room, for lofts, flats and offices of up to about 150 m².

  • Furnished plan at 1:50
  • Storage and joinery layout
  • Lighting positions
  • Material board
  • Shopping list with dimensions
  • One revision round included

02Questions

Before you write.

Short answers for this fictional studio.

Do you work outside the city?

In this demo, yes, within about two hours of Sample City. Site visits outside that range are quoted separately.

Can you work from my own sketch?

Yes. A sketch, a phone photo of the plot and a list of rooms is enough to begin a conversation.

How is the fee calculated?

A fixed fee per phase, agreed after the first consultation. You see the number before any work starts.

Is this studio real?

No. Studio Alder is invented for a web development portfolio. The drawings are illustrations created for the demo.
