02How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

You see a decision before it becomes a cost. Each step ends with a drawing and a short note on what changed.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We visit the plot or the flat, listen, and write a one-page brief you can sign off.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Two or three layouts on one sheet, with the trade-offs written beside each.

  3. 03

    Detail

    The chosen plan becomes drawings that a builder can price and build from.

  4. 04

    On site

    We answer questions during the build and check the work against the drawings.
