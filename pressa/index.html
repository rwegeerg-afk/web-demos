The lineup

Crate to bottle
in four hours

Every bottle you get was fruit at dawn. Here is one ordinary weekday at the studio.

  1. Step 1 · Arrive05:30

    Crates come in

    Fruit from three farms in the Oeste region, picked the day before.

  2. Step 2 · Prep07:00

    Washed and cut

    By hand. Peel stays on where it adds flavour, comes off where it turns bitter.

  3. Step 3 · Press08:15

    Two tonnes of press

    A hydraulic press squeezes the pulp in cloth bags. No blades, no heat, no air whipped in.

  4. Step 4 · Chill10:00

    Bottled at 4 °C

    Into glass, onto ice, into the van. Drink within 72 hours for full flavour.
