How it works

Four steps, and you stay informed at each one.

  1. 1

    Send your model

    STL, 3MF or STEP. No model yet? Send a sketch with sizes and we will say what is possible.

  2. 2

    Get a quote

    You receive a fixed price, material suggestion and delivery date. No surprises later.

    We print and check

    Every part is measured against the model and inspected for defects before packing.

    Delivery

    Parts are packed to survive the trip. Tracking number comes by email.

Questions people ask

Which file formats do you accept?

STL, 3MF and STEP. If you only have a drawing or a photo with sizes, tell us in the form and we will see what can be done.

How accurate are the parts?

Typical tolerance is about ±0.2 mm on FDM plastics and ±0.1 mm on resin. We tell you before printing if a feature is too tight to hold.

Can you keep my design private?

Yes. Files are used for your order only and deleted after delivery unless you ask us to keep them.

What if a part fails?

If the fault is ours, we reprint it at no cost. If the design has a problem, we explain what to change.
